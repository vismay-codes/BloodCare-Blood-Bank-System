OUR MISSION

Why BloodCare?

BloodCare is designed to make blood-bank operations easier to monitor by connecting donor discovery, blood stock visibility and emergency request handling in one workspace.

Faster donor discoverySearch by blood group and city while respecting donor eligibility.
Smarter stock visibilityTrack available units and identify stock approaching expiry.
Priority-driven responseCritical and urgent requests rise through the priority engine.
